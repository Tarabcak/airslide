AirSlide example deck

Swing an AirPod.
Change the slide.

Turn your wrist right for the next slide, left to go back. Arrow keys work too.

How it works

One presenter, every screen.

  1. The Mac app reads your AirPod's motion and recognises a deliberate swing.
  2. It sends next or prev to the relay with your private key.
  3. The relay forwards it to every browser showing this deck.

Bring your own deck

Any HTML deck that moves on arrow keys.

Start the relay with your REMOTE_TOKEN and DECK_DIR=/path/to/deck. Decks with their own API can define window.airslideNavigate.

Before you present

Rehearse on this Mac first.

Local rehearsal sends gestures to 127.0.0.1:8094, so you can practise without touching the live deck.
